Raycall Support & FAQ

Need help with Raycall (com.weifengluo.raycall)? Find answers to common questions below or reach out to our team directly.

Q1: Does Raycall upload my photos or require an internet connection?

No. Raycall ships with the Google EmbeddingGemma 2 (440M LiteRT) model bundled inside the app. All indexing, OCR text recognition, and semantic search happen 100% offline on your iPhone.

Q2: How do I index my entire photo library faster?

By default, Raycall indexes gently in the foreground to keep your iPhone cool, and finishes indexing overnight while locked on a charger. If you want to index your library immediately at maximum speed, tap the amber "Boost Now" button on the Home screen or inside Settings & Index Engine. Tap again at any time to pause boost.

Q3: How do I copy text from an ID card, receipt, or screenshot?

Tap any search result to open the full-screen viewer. When text is detected in the image, an interactive Recognized Text (OCR) card appears at the bottom. Tap Expand All to select specific words or tap Share Text to copy or share the recognized text.

Q4: How do I find visually similar photos?

Open any photo or video and tap Similar in the top-right corner (or Find Similar in the bottom bar) to instantly match other photos in your library using 768D visual embeddings.
