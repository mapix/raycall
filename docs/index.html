Engineered for Speed, Precision & Complete Privacy

Every component of Raycall is tuned to run directly on Apple Silicon — Metal GPU, Neural Engine, and SQLite WAL.

✨

768D Multimodal Embeddings

Bundled with Google EmbeddingGemma 2 (440M LiteRT). Encodes visual scenes, timestamps, seasons, and offline location names into a shared 768-dimensional vector space.

🪪

Instant OCR for IDs & Receipts

Pre-screens photos with Apple Vision text rectangle detection and runs high-accuracy OCR on documents, boarding passes, invoices, and screenshots with 1-tap text sharing.

🎯

One-Tap Visual Similarity

Found one great shot? Tap "Find Similar" on any photo or video to instantly surface visually and semantically related moments across your entire camera roll.

⚡

Matryoshka Two-Stage Retrieval

Scans thousands of memories in milliseconds using 256D coarse filtering followed by full 768D cosine reranking and exact OCR keyword boosting.

🌡️

Thermal-Aware & Boost Mode

Deduplicates burst shots via Neural Engine feature prints, throttles automatically if your iPhone warms up, and offers instant 1-tap Boost Mode whenever you want full speed.

🔒

Hidden Album Isolation

Strictly respects iOS photo permissions. Items inside your system "Hidden" and "Recently Deleted" albums are never read or indexed.
